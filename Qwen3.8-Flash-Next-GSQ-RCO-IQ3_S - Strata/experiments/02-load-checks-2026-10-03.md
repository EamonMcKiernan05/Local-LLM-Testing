# 02 — One card vs two, and the 256K context check (2026-10-03)

The day the base IQ3_S was installed and the storage re-architecture landed. Two checks:
how much the second card buys, and whether the full 262,144-token window actually fits.

## One card vs two — before and after the NVMe re-home

The first single-card test barely ran: an 8K prompt took **364 s** at 25.7 tok/s prefill — only
~51 % of the expert set fitted the card's cache, and the rest streamed from the spinning disk at
~73 MB/s, with a ~6-minute cold-load penalty on the first request after every load. The fix was
the storage policy this box now runs: the NVMe volume was extended (98 → 232 GB) and the base
model's hot files were copied onto it with symlinks left at the standard paths (pack + shard 1 +
the n-gram; the HDD keeps `.hdd-original` copies as the cold catalogue).

Same tests, same prompts, after the re-home:

| config | 8k — read / decode (tok/s) | 100k — read / decode (tok/s) |
|---|---|---|
| single card, pre-NVMe (cold, HDD-streamed) | 25.7 / 19.4 | 701.6 / 32.6 |
| single card, post-NVMe (+ 16 GiB RAM tier) | 590.9 / 37.3 | 834.2 / 41.4 |
| two cards, pre-NVMe (best repeatable) | 845.1 / 80.0 | 1,689.5 / 60.1 |
| two cards, post-NVMe | 842.9 / 65.6 | 1,677.0 / 65.5 |

Two readings:

- **The second card roughly doubles prompt processing at depth** — 834 → 1,677 tok/s at 100k —
  and lifts single-card decode from 41.4 to 65.5 tok/s. With both cards' caches loaded they
  together cover the whole expert set, which is why every later benchmark reports ~100 % decode
  cache hits.
- **The NVMe re-home rescued the single-card numbers** (8k prefill 25.7 → 590.9 tok/s) and cut
  model load time from 6.7 to ~1.5 minutes.

## 256K context verified on both cards

The base model ships 262,144 tokens of context; this is the run that proved the two cards can
hold it. A **250,592-token prompt** through the full window:

> `strata serve: prompt 250592 tokens = 0 reused + 250592 read in 168387 ms (1488.2 tok/s), 62
> generated in 1195 ms (51.9 tok/s), drafts accepted 40 of 49`

Load: 12,800 + 11,677 expert slots across the two cards, 2.6 GB VRAM spare. Engine log line in
[`engine-log-extracts.txt`](../data/raw/engine-log-extracts.txt).
