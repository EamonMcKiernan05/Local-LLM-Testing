## Reading — the mid-range prefill gain is the baseline's own wander

The agreement check says it plainly: the fresh 0.1.42 re-run against its own morning run (same
engine, same config, same day) landed −5.6 / −7.4 / −8.0 % on prefill at 100K / 150K / 200K (and
±1 % elsewhere) — exactly the depths where the pair reads +6–9 %. Against the morning's own
v0.1.42 numbers the two engines agree within **±0.5 % at 20–200K prefill** (−1.3 % at 250K).

Decode moves inside the established ±5–8 % single-rep band (mean −2.1 %; the worst cell is
200K's −7.9 %, mirrored by the re-run's decode sitting high there vs its morning run).

So: **no measurable speed change from v0.1.43** — the release is behavioural (high-occupancy
tail-skip on the V100s and the Linux overlap default), which is what its notes say.
