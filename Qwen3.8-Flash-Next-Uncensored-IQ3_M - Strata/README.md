# Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-Uncensored (IQ3_M) on Strata — 262K context, box .5

**2 recorded runs (12 measured arms), 0 failed.** The third-party **Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-Uncensored-IQ3_M**
bake — 2 shards on the SN720 NVMe — served by **Strata v0.2.3.1** (engine 0.1.48.1, local sm_70 build) on
**2× Tesla V100 32 GB**, measured **2026-10-24** on box .5: **262,144 context**, KV int8,
`--mmap-experts`, two-card layer split, vision on, the model's own converted MTP drafter active.

| depth | prefill tok/s (mean of 2) | decode tok/s (mean of 2) |
|---|---:|---:|
| 20K | 1,095.5 | 70.05 |
| 60K | 1,389.7 | 68.00 |
| 100K | 1,451.4 | 64.80 |
| 150K | 1,477.5 | 64.45 |
| 200K | 1,461.7 | 62.70 |
| 250K | 1,431.9 | 61.75 |

**The findings:**

- **Prefill climbs to a peak of 1,477.5 tok/s at 150K** and then eases back to 1,431.9 at 250K — the curve is
  a hump, not a plateau, and the peak sits at half the context window.
- **Decode is the flattest on the box across this depth range**: 70.05 tok/s at 20K sliding to 61.75 at 250K,
  a 12 % loss across a 230K-token deeper context. That is what `--mmap-experts` with the shards on the NVMe
  and a two-card layer split buys.
- **Both runs are on one load**, back to back, after a discarded 100K burn. This is therefore a
  warm-cache measurement of the model's steady state, not a fresh-load number, and it should not be compared
  against a fresh-load figure from another batch without saying so.
- Reproducibility between the two runs is tight in prefill (within 1 % at every depth) and tight in decode
  from 60K on (within 1 %), with one exception worth naming: the 20K cell reads 67.9 and 72.2 tok/s, 6 %
  apart, on the same load. That is the first measured cell of the pair and the most cache-sensitive.
