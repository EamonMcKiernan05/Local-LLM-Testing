## What is raw, what is derived

- **Raw** (`data/raw/`): the twelve model-bench run dirs as the harness wrote them — `results.jsonl`,
  `meta.json`, `summary.csv` — copied unchanged and covered by `SHA256SUMS`. The mapping from published
  name to original run directory is in `scripts/runs.txt`, so every row can be traced back to a dir name.
- **Derived** (`data/csv/`): `averages.csv` (3-run mean and per-run min/max per arm per depth),
  `means.csv` (the per-run rows the card reads), `facts.json`. Regenerated by `scripts/build_data.py`,
  which asserts 12 runs, 3 per arm, and the same document at each depth within a group.
- **Chart** (`charts/`): rendered from `data/csv/means.csv`; no figure typed by hand.

## Which numbers these are

- **Maya v1.1.45**, engine `maya-ds4`, pack `…/UD-IQ1_S/maya-ds4`, `--max-context 131072 --eos 1
  --vision off --tp 2 --prefill-chunk 16384 --cpu-threads 20`, drafter arms `on + --suffix-draft 3` and
  `none + --suffix-draft 0`.
- **Strata 0.2.9**, engine `strata-dsv41` (hand-built for sm_70 from the 0.2.9 tree — upstream packages are
  sm_86/89/120 only), pack `…/UD-IQ1_S/maya-ds4`, same context and tuning flags, `--dspark on|none`.
- Both packs were rebuilt to carry Maya's 4.25 GB `dspark-xs2` drafter (1,245 mtp tensors) before this
  battery; before that rebuild Strata logged zero drafts and the flag silently did nothing.
- `model-bench` 0.1.0, 400 tokens generated, temperature 0.5, seed 12345, one rep per depth.

## Hardware and residency

2× Tesla V100-PCIE-32GB, 31 GB system RAM, box .5. Both engines loaded from the **NVMe**
(`/mnt/nvme/ds41/…/UD-IQ1_S`); the set is the same 284,640,120,584 bytes as the HDD master under
`/mnt/models`. Both sit at **6,705 of 15,360** routed experts in VRAM, with 8,955 experts (67.5 GB)
streaming in place — that lane is the whole story of the decode gap.

## What could not be reached / what is not covered

- **The Strata side has not been re-measured on the 5 Nov batch.** Strata 0.2.9 is the last measured Strata
  figure for this model file; the newer batch only re-ran Maya (on v1.1.45 → v1.1.53) and added the EXL3
  quant. Do not read a Strata number as "today's" — read its date.
- **No as-shipped arm in this battery.** Both engines ran the tuned configuration the 31 Oct sweep picked
  (`--prefill-chunk 16384 --cpu-threads 20`); an earlier same-day sweep on 1 Nov holds as-shipped runs
  (`2026-11-01T1044Z`, `T1057Z` and the `tuned-drafter` pair at 1320/1332Z) and was deliberately excluded
  here: this folder records the 12-run controlled battery, not the whole day.
- **Decode is noisy on both engines and the spread is published**, not smoothed: Maya drafter-off at 100K
  spans 9.7–12.9 tok/s across its three runs. Differences under ~15 % decode are not evidence of anything.
- **The V100 EXL3 path did not exist in this pair.** Strata 0.2.9 and Maya v1.1.45 are GGUF-path engines for
  this model; the EXL3 quant is measured in the sibling batch on v1.1.53 only.
